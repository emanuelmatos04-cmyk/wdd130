Nephi Rafting

Customer Experience

Our rafting adventure in Jarabacoa with Nephi Rafting was, without a doubt, the highlight of our trip. From the moment we stepped onto the Yaque del Norte River, we knew a serious dose of adrenaline was coming. Our guides were professional and incredibly fun, making us feel safe while we navigated exciting rapids like the famous 'Tyson'. The combination of the cold mountain waters with the stunning green canyons of the 'Dominican Alps' created an unforgettable experience. We are definitely coming back for more.

History

Our rafting company's history begins in 1985, when a group of friends passionate about the river decided to share their love for adventure with the world. Since then, we have grown from a small local operation into an industry leader, always maintaining our commitment to safety, fun, and environmental conservation. Every trip is a new page in our continuous adventure.

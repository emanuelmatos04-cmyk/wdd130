Featured Trips

Beginner Rapids Adventure

Perfect for families and first-time rafters, this trip takes you through gentle rapids with breathtaking views of the Dominican Alps. Our guides ensure safety while you enjoy the thrill of rafting at a comfortable pace.

Intermediate Challenge

For those seeking more excitement, the Intermediate Challenge offers moderate rapids and longer runs. Navigate through canyon walls and enjoy the adrenaline rush while still keeping the adventure accessible.

Extreme Rapids Expedition

Designed for thrill-seekers, this expedition takes you through the most intense rapids on the Yaque del Norte River. With expert guides and top safety gear, you’ll conquer waves and drops for the ultimate rafting experience.
